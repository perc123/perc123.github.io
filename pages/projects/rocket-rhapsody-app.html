rocket rhapsody — app

The interactive Unity application built for the Rocket Rhapsody concert, letting the audience co-create the visuals live: attendees picked an avatar, applied effects and colors through DAW-inspired controls, drew animations, and watched their contributions rendered into the concert's closing credit-roll video. Twenty-five-plus scripts, organized by subsystem (scroll navigation, UI, animation, video playback, avatar management, credits), built on Unity 2021 LTS with the new Input System, UI Toolkit/TextMeshPro, and VFX Graph — with UI designs exported from Figma as structured JSON to speed up designer-developer handoff ahead of the event.
